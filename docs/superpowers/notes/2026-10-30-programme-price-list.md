## Limits

- **Tier per student is unknown.** A seat count cannot say whether a student paid the self-funded or the HRDC price. `deal.funding_type` (or the LMS funding column) can, and the who-pays chart already reads it.
- **Older products** (Mar to Jun 2026 workshops such as Codex, Marketing AI Automation and OpenClaw) have no page in the list and no price here.
- **Not read:** the "LEAD programs intention form" (`thelead.io/program`) and the JooY ticket link for the 13 Oct workshop, which returned "Page Not Found".
- **Revenue from these prices is an estimate**, seats times list price. It ignores discounts, promos, refunds and unpaid seats. Label any chart built on it as estimated until real payments are imported.
